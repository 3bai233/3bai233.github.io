Selected Publications

Research at the intersection of multimodal reasoning and real-world interaction.

NeurIPS 2026 · PosterCo-first author (listed first)

AmbiguousWorld: Benchmarking and Resolving Ambiguous Instructions in Video World Models

Bo Wang* et al.

Studies how video world models resolve ambiguous instructions. I led the research project under Prof. Changshui Zhang and proposed a multimodal Tree-of-Thought framework using VLM-guided subgoal decomposition and candidate branch selection. The approach improves weighted scores by 17.94–24.37 points across three video generators.

NeurIPS poster
arXiv · Nov 2026Co-first author (listed second)

HEIR: Harness Egocentric Intent for Human-Robot Interactions

Yichi Zhang*, Bo Wang*, Zhenghan Zhu*, et al.

A human–robot interaction dataset aligning speech, gaze and egocentric video with robot observations and navigation–manipulation trajectories. HEIR covers 300 interaction sessions and 6,416 subtasks. I contributed throughout the research, built the execution and evaluation harness, and conducted real-robot deployment and testing on the Galaxea R1 Lite.

PaperProjectDataset
CVPR 2026Co-first author (listed third)

BAMI: Training-Free Bias Mitigation in GUI Grounding

Borui Zhang*, Bo Zhang*, Bo Wang*, et al.

A training-free approach for precise GUI grounding. I developed masked prediction distribution (MPD) attribution and mask-guided dynamic cropping to mitigate grounding bias. BAMI improves TianXi-Action-7B from 51.9% to 57.8% on ScreenSpot-Pro.
